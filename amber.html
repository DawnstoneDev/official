01 / The Idea

Music,
without the noise.

Most players want your attention — feeds, suggestions, badges. Amber wants none of that.

It plays your music, and gets out of the way.

02 / Native

Made for
Mac.

Amber is built entirely in Swift and SwiftUI, designed for macOS Tahoe, with an interface that draws on Liquid Glass to feel like it belongs on your Mac — not on top of it.

Swift SwiftUI macOS Tahoe Liquid Glass

Drag your music in, and Amber takes care of the rest — organizing titles, artists, albums and artwork into a library that stays out of your way until you need it.

06 / Audio

Listen
your way.

Amber is being built with real attention to how it sounds, not just how it looks — with room to shape playback in detail as the app matures, for the people who care about the difference.

07 / Philosophy

A player that
stays out of the way.

Amber isn't trying to be the star. The music is.

Every part of it — the interface, the animation, the sound — is built to disappear behind whatever you're listening to.
