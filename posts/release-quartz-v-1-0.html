Quartz is here.

We built a small utility for macOS — quiet enough to disappear, useful enough to keep around.

August 2026 Quartz Release 001

Quartz began with a simple idea: not every Mac app needs to become a workspace.

Some tools should simply do one small thing, do it well, and get out of the way. No dashboard. No account. No complicated setup. Just a small window, a focused utility, and a moment where the thing you needed is already done.

That's what Quartz is. It's the second generation of a direction we've been exploring at Dawnstone: small, native applications that respect the space around them.

Small by design.

Quartz isn't trying to become another application that lives permanently in the background of your Mac. For version 1.0, it's intentionally a normal application. Open it when you need it. Use it. Close it.

The window is small and vertical, because the utility itself is small. We wanted the interface to feel closer to a physical instrument than a traditional desktop application: compact, immediate, and easy to understand.

macOS Version 1.0

Native, quietly.

Like the other Dawnstone projects, Quartz is built with the Mac in mind first. We didn't want a web wrapper that happened to run on macOS. We wanted something that feels like it belongs there.

That means a small window, familiar interactions, proper macOS typography, and an interface that doesn't need to explain itself every time you open it.

"The best utility is often the one you barely notice using."

That's probably the most important decision we made while building Quartz. We removed things more often than we added them. If something didn't make the utility faster, clearer, or calmer, it probably didn't belong.

Version one.

Quartz 1.0 is deliberately small. This first release is about establishing the foundation rather than filling every possible corner with features.

Future versions may grow, but they should grow carefully. We would rather keep Quartz focused than turn a small utility into another application that demands your attention.

This is the beginning of Quartz, not the final definition of it. For now, though, it's ready. A small piece of software, built for the Mac, designed to stay quiet.
