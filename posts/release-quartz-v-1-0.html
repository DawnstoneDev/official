Quartz is here.

A small, quiet system information app for macOS — designed to show you what your Mac is doing without getting in the way.

August 2026 Quartz v1.0

Quartz began with a simple idea: system information shouldn't need to feel like system information.

There are plenty of utilities that can tell you almost everything about your Mac. Processor details, memory pressure, storage, battery health, displays, network interfaces — all of it is available somewhere.

But sometimes you don't need everything. You just want to know how your Mac is doing. That's where Quartz comes in.

Just enough information.

Quartz focuses on the small set of information that is useful at a glance. CPU usage. Memory. Storage. Battery. Display. Network status.

Nothing is hidden behind a collection of complicated dashboards or diagnostic panels. Open Quartz, look, and close it again.

  • CPU, memory, and storage at a glance
  • Basic hardware and display information
  • Battery and charging status
  • Simple network connectivity information

Quiet by design.

The interface is intentionally restrained. Soft surfaces, large spacing, subtle translucency, and a small amount of information hierarchy keep the window readable without turning it into a dashboard.

We wanted Quartz to feel like something that belongs on macOS rather than another utility that happens to run on it.

"You shouldn't have to manage the tool you're using to understand your Mac."

Built to disappear.

Quartz isn't meant to become another application you keep open all day. It's a small utility for those moments when you want a little more context about your Mac, without opening Activity Monitor or digging through System Settings.

Open it. Take a look. Get what you need. Then get back to whatever you were doing.

Version one.

Quartz v1.0 is our first public release. It is intentionally small, and that's something we want to preserve as the project grows.

Future versions may add more useful pieces of system information, but the principle will remain the same: show enough, stay quiet, and get out of the way.
