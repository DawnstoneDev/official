01 / The Idea

A map,
not a feed.

Most writing tools flatten everything into one long scroll. Atlas treats what you write as a structure — something you can see the shape of, not just read from top to bottom.

It gives your knowledge a place to sit still.

02 / The Format

Plain text.
Fully yours.

.atlas is a Markdown-based format at its core — readable anywhere, owned by no one. Headings, links, code, images and metadata are held together as one document, without locking your writing inside an app.

03 / Structure

Every document
has a shape.

Headings become an outline. Links become connections. References become a trail you can follow back. Atlas reads the structure already inside your writing, and lets you move through it.

Outline Backlinks Metadata Cross-references

04 / Editor

Made for
Mac.

Atlas is built entirely in Swift and SwiftUI, designed for macOS Tahoe, with an interface that draws on Liquid Glass to feel like it belongs on your Mac — not on top of it.

07 / Philosophy

A tool that
stays out of the way.

Atlas isn't trying to be the star. Your thinking is.

Every part of it — the interface, the animation, the structure — is built to disappear behind whatever you're working through.
